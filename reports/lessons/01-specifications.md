## What a specification is, and why these need one

Every lesson is checked against a **specification entry**: the skill's name, its prerequisites, and
its *demonstration*, the task that shows the skill fully. The importer refuses a lesson whose name
or prerequisites drift from the entry. V3's entries were reviewed in its design pass; these 23
master skills never had one. So each entry here is built as follows:
- **name and prerequisites** are copied from the map, so they cannot drift;
- **the assessment wording** for each mode (cases required, acceptance, evidence record) is V3's own,
  copied from its specification;
- **only the demonstration is new.** For the 17 `sss-rob-*` skills it is built from their existing
  "I can…" statement and scope note.

Each entry is marked `master_skill: true`. The importer will match it by the skill's own id, and no
planning id is added to the skill.

## The 23 demonstrations

Mode is *written calculation* unless marked (CE = *calculation and explanation*).

| Skill | Demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| `sss-rob-serial-dof` | Count the configuration coordinates of stated fixed-base open chains, label each as an angle or a translation, and identify a case (a closed loop or a coupled joint) where the counting rule does not apply. |
| `p-charge` (CE) | Track charge through stated transfers between objects and around a circuit, using conservation and whole multiples of e, and explain why a circuit converts energy rather than consuming charge. |
| `sss-rob-frame-labels` | Label the frame of each coordinate tuple in a multi-sensor scenario, flag every comparison across frames, and explain how different coordinates can describe the same unmoved object. |
| `sss-rob-joint-conventions` | Write a configuration vector with joint order, units, signs and zero references, and convert its angles between relative and absolute form. |
| `sss-rob-rotation-columns` | Build R_AB from frame B's axes expressed in A, use it to convert B components into A, and show that swapping the labels gives the transpose. |
| `sss-rob-task-coordinates` | Say whether a target specifies position, orientation or both, distinguish it from a joint configuration, and explain why reaching a point does not guarantee every orientation there. |
| `sss-rob-point-direction` | Convert a point and a free displacement vector between frames, and show that the difference of two converted points rotates without the translation. |
| `sss-rob-rotation-check` | Test matrices for R^T R = I and det R = +1 within a tolerance, and classify each as a rotation, a reflection or a scaled matrix. |
| `m-log` | Evaluate and rearrange logarithms with the definition, the product, quotient and power rules and change of base; state domain limits; reject splitting log(x + y). |
| `sss-rob-planar-reach` | Test targets against abs(L1 - L2) <= r <= L1 + L2, and state what a pass does not establish (limits, obstacles, orientation). |
| `sss-rob-compose` | Compose a labeled chain T_AC = T_AB T_BC in the correct order, and check it with a point of known location. |
| `sss-rob-inverse` | Invert a rigid transform as R^T and -R^T t, and verify it with a point round trip. |
| `sss-rob-planar-fk` | Compute the tip position and orientation of an ideal planar two-link arm, and check the straight and right-angle configurations. |
| `m-trigidentity` | Simplify expressions and prove identities with the Pythagorean, quotient, sum and double-angle identities, and check each numerically at a test angle. |
| `p-electricfield` (CE) | Calculate the force between point charges and the field of one or two point charges with direction, and the force on a charge of either sign in a given field. |
| `sss-rob-planar-ik` | Compute both regular IK branches for a reachable target, and identify the boundary and degenerate cases. |
| `sss-rob-tool-offset` | Append a fixed link-to-tool transform and compute the tool point in the base frame, rotating a local offset first. |
| `p-potentialelectric` (CE) | Calculate energy transfers with dU = q dV, relate potential difference to a uniform field with dV = E d, and state the zero reference. |
| `sss-rob-ik-verification` | Verify IK candidates by forward substitution and joint limits, reject each failure with its reason, and report which checks were and were not done. |
| `m-transdiff` | Differentiate exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions with the chain rule in radians, and explain the factor that appears in degrees. |
| `sss-rob-position-jacobian` | Derive the 2x2 position Jacobian of a planar two-link arm, and state what its rows and columns mean. |
| `sss-rob-joint-to-tip-rate` | Calculate tip velocity from joint rates at the current configuration, and show the same rates give a different velocity elsewhere. |
| `sss-rob-planar-singularity` | Identify the singular configurations from det J = L1 L2 sin(q2), and describe which instantaneous tip motion is lost and which remains. |
